テーマ：10.交通 · Topic: Transportation (Japanese)

10. kōtsū

バイリンガル会話ガイド

Participation

How do I participate in the conversation table?

Use the “Raise your hand” button under the video. The host will call on you to unmute your microphone and will let as many students participate as possible during the allotted time.

What if I don’t know how to answer?

No worries! If we call on you and you're not sure how to answer, just stay quiet and we'll move on to someone else. Feel free to answer in the chat if that's easier!

01

はじめに

Hajime ni

Getting Started

1

お名前は何ですか?

o namae wa nani desu ka?

What is your name?

2

どこに住んでいますか?

doko ni sun de i masu ka?

Where do you live?

3

今日は、何で移動しましたか?

kyō wa, nande idō shi mashi ta ka?

How did you travel today?

02

毎日の移動

Mainichi no idō

Your Daily Travel

4

いつも歩きますか、車を運転しますか、それとも電車やバスに乗りますか?

itsumo aruki masu ka, kuruma o unten shi masu ka, soretomo densha ya basu ni nori masu ka?

Do you usually walk, drive, or take public transportation?

5

あなたの家族は、いつも何で移動しますか?

anata no kazoku wa, itsumo nani de idō shi masu ka?

How do people in your family usually travel?

6

いつも何で、学校や仕事、他の場所に行きますか?

itsumo nande, gakkō ya shigoto, ta no basho ni iki masu ka?

How do you usually go to school, work, or other places?

7

あなたの家族は、どんな乗り物を使いますか?

anata no kazoku wa, donna norimono o tsukai masu ka?

What transportation do people in your family use?

8

あなたの住んでいるところでは、みんなよく歩きますか?

anata no sun de iru tokoro de wa, minna yoku aruki masu ka?

Do people walk a lot where you live?

03

地域の交通

Chiiki no kōtsū

Transportation in Your Community

9

みんなは、バス、電車、タクシー、自転車、車を使いますか?

minna wa, basu, densha, takushī, jitensha, kuruma o tsukai masu ka?

Do people use buses, trains, taxis, bikes, or cars?

10

あなたの住んでいるところでは、交通は便利ですか?

anata no sun de iru tokoro de wa, kōtsū wa benri desu ka?

Is transportation easy in your community?

11

交通費は高いですか?

kōtsū hi wa takai desu ka?

Is transportation expensive?

12

何時ごろ、電車やバスは混みますか?

nan ji goro, densha ya basu wa komi masu ka?

What time is transportation busy?

13

kodomo wa, ichi nin de gakkō ni iki masu ka, soretomo dare ka to iki masu ka?

Do children go to school alone or with someone?

14

大人は、いつも何で移動しますか?

otona wa, itsumo nani de idō shi masu ka?

How do older people usually travel?

15

kazoku wa, issho ni dō yatte idō shi masu ka?

16

遠くに行くとき、みんなは何を使いますか?

tōku ni iku toki, minna wa nani o tsukai masu ka?

What transportation do people use for long trips?

17

近くに行くとき、みんなは何を使いますか?

chikaku ni iku toki, minna wa nani o tsukai masu ka?

What transportation do people use for short trips?

18

What is one transportation problem in your community?

19

どの乗り物が一番好きですか?
