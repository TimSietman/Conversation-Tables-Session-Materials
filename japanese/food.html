6. tabemono

バイリンガル会話ガイド

Participation

How do I participate in the conversation table?

Use the “Raise your hand” button under the video. The host will call on you to unmute your microphone and will let as many students participate as possible during the allotted time.

What if I don’t know how to answer?

No worries! If we call on you and you're not sure how to answer, just stay quiet and we'll move on to someone else. Feel free to answer in the chat if that's easier!

02

Your Preferences and Daily Meals

4

What food do you not like?

5

6

asa gohan ni, itsumo nani o tabe masu ka?

What do you usually eat for breakfast?

7

お昼ごはんに、いつも何を食べますか?

ohiru gohan ni, itsumo nani o tabe masu ka?

What do you usually eat for lunch?

8

晩ごはんに、いつも何を食べますか?

ban gohan ni, itsumo nani o tabe masu ka?

What do you usually eat for dinner?

03

家族との食事

Kazoku to no shokuji

Eating with Family

9

あなたの家族が一緒に食べるのが好きな食べ物は何ですか?

anata no kazoku ga issho ni taberu no ga suki na tabemono wa nani desu ka?

What food does your family like to eat together?

10

あなたの家や家族では、いつも誰が料理をしますか?

anata no ie ya kazoku de wa, itsumo dare ga ryōri o shi masu ka?

Who usually cooks in your home or family?

11

いつも誰が食べ物を買いますか?

itsumo dare ga tabemono o kai masu ka?

Who usually buys food?

12

あなたの家の人は、一緒に食べますか、それとも別々の時間に食べますか?

anata no ie no hito wa, issho ni tabe masu ka, soretomo betsubetsu no jikan ni tabe masu ka?

Do people in your home eat together or at different times?

13

家族の食事は、静かですか、忙しいですか、きちんとしていますか、リラックスしていますか?

kazoku no shokuji wa, shizuka desu ka, isogashii desu ka, kichinto shi te i masu ka, rirakkusu shi te i masu ka?

Are family meals quiet, busy, formal, or relaxed?

14

特別な日には、どんな食べ物を食べますか?

tokubetsu na hi ni wa, donna tabemono o tabe masu ka?

What food do you eat on special days?

04

食べ物と特別な行事

Tabemono to tokubetsu na gyōji

Food and Special Occasions

15

家族やまわりの人たちのイベントには、食べ物を持っていきますか?

kazoku ya mawari no hito tachi no ibento ni wa, tabemono o motte iki masu ka?

Do people bring food to family or community events?

16

あなたの家族やまわりの子供は、どんな食べ物が好きですか?

anata no kazoku ya mawari no kodomo wa, donna tabemono ga suki desu ka?

What food do children in your family or community like?

17

あなたの家族やまわりの大人は、どんな食べ物が好きですか?

anata no kazoku ya mawari no otona wa, donna tabemono ga suki desu ka?

What food do older people in your family or community like?

18

家族から教わって食べるようになった食べ物はありますか?

kazoku kara osowatte taberu yō ni natta tabemono wa ari masu ka?

Is there a food you learned to eat from your family?

19

家に来た人に、どんな食べ物をあげますか?

ie ni ki ta hito ni, donna tabemono o age masu ka?

What is one food custom in your family or community?

21

anata no kuni de yūmei na tabemono wa nani desu ka?

What food is famous in your country?

22

What meal or food tradition is important in your country?
