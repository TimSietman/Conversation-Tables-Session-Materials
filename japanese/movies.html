12. eiga

バイリンガル会話ガイド

Participation

How do I participate in the conversation table?

Use the “Raise your hand” button under the video. The host will call on you to unmute your microphone and will let as many students participate as possible during the allotted time.

What if I don’t know how to answer?

No worries! If we call on you and you're not sure how to answer, just stay quiet and we'll move on to someone else. Feel free to answer in the chat if that's easier!

01

はじめに

Hajime ni

Getting Started

1

お名前は何ですか?

o namae wa nani desu ka?

What is your name?

2

出身はどこですか?

shusshin wa doko desu ka?

Where are you from?

3

映画は好きですか?

eiga wa suki desu ka?

Do you like movies?

02

好みと習慣

Konomi to shūkan

Your Preferences and Habits

4

どんな映画が好きですか?

donna eiga ga suki desu ka?

What kind of movie do you like?

5

映画は、家族と見ますか、友達と見ますか、それとも一人で見ますか?

eiga wa, kazoku to mi masu ka, tomodachi to mi masu ka, soretomo ichi nin de mi masu ka?

Do you watch movies with family, friends, or alone?

6

どのくらいよく映画を見ますか?

dono kurai yoku eiga o mi masu ka?

How often do you watch movies?

7

いつも、どこで映画を見ますか?

itsumo, doko de eiga o mi masu ka?

Where do you usually watch movies?

8

映画館に行きますか?

eiga kan ni iki masu ka?

Do you go to movie theaters?

03

家族と見る映画

Kazoku to miru eiga

Movies with Family

9

いつも、誰と映画を見ますか?

itsumo, dare to eiga o mi masu ka?

Who do you usually watch movies with?

10

あなたの家族は、どんな映画が好きですか?

anata no kazoku wa, donna eiga ga suki desu ka?

What kind of movies does your family like?

11

あなたの家族やまわりの人たちの子供は、どんな映画が好きですか?

anata no kazoku ya mawari no hito tachi no kodomo wa, donna eiga ga suki desu ka?

What movies do children in your family or community like?

12

大人は、どんな映画が好きですか?

otona wa, donna eiga ga suki desu ka?

What movies do older people like?

13

映画を見るとき、みんなは何か食べますか?

eiga o miru toki, minna wa nani ka tabe masu ka?

Do people eat food during movies?

14

家で映画を見るとき、みんなは話しますか?

ie de eiga o miru toki, minna wa hanashi masu ka?

Do people talk during movies at home?

04

思い出と文化

Omoide to bunka

Memories and Culture

15

みんなは、英語、あなたの言語、それとも他の言語で映画を見ますか?

minna wa, eigo, anata no gengo, soretomo ta no gengo de eiga o mi masu ka?

Do people watch movies in English, your language, or another language?

16

字幕を使いますか?

jimaku o tsukai masu ka?

Do you use subtitles?

17

子供のとき、どんな映画が好きでしたか?

kodomo no toki, donna eiga ga suki deshi ta ka?

What movie did you like as a child?

18

あなたの家族が、何度も見る映画はありますか?

anata no kazoku ga, nan do mo miru eiga wa ari masu ka?

Is there a movie your family watches again and again?

19

どの映画から、あなたの国の文化が分かりますか?

dono eiga kara, anata no kuni no bunka ga wakari masu ka?

What movie can teach about your community or culture?

05

あなたの国の映画

Anata no kuni no eiga

Movies in Your Country

20

あなたの家族には、映画を見るときのいつもの習慣がありますか?

anata no kazoku ni wa, eiga o miru toki no itsumo no shūkan ga ari masu ka?

What is one movie habit in your family?

21

あなたの国で、人気の映画は何ですか?

anata no kuni de, ninki no eiga wa nani desu ka?

What movies are popular in your country?

22

あなたの国で、有名な映画は何ですか?有名な俳優や監督は誰ですか?

anata no kuni de, yūmei na eiga wa nani desu ka? yūmei na haiyū ya kantoku wa dare desu ka?

What movie, actor, or director is famous in your country?

✓

まとめとあいさつ

Matome to aisatsu

Conclusion and goodbye.
