TalkAbroad Conversation Tables

Topic: Making a Popular Dish (Mandarin Chinese)

Guided cultural activity · English permitted

Participation

How do I participate in the conversation table?

Use the “Raise your hand” button under the video. The host will call on you to unmute your microphone and will let as many students participate as possible during the allotted time.

What if I don’t know how to answer?

No worries! If we call on you and you're not sure how to answer, just stay quiet and we'll move on to someone else. Feel free to answer in the chat if that's easier!

English is permitted in this session. You may use English or Mandarin Chinese to ask questions and participate in the activity.

05

常用句型chángyòng jùxíng

Common sentence patterns · Mandarin / English

今天我来做一个……jīntiān wǒ lái zuò yí ge……today I’m going to make a…
我把……洗干净。wǒ bǎ…… xǐ gānjìng.I washed the ... clean.
我切……wǒ qiē……i cut the …
打开炉灶。dǎkāi lúzào.turn on the stove.
现在我开始炒菜。xiànzài wǒ kāishǐ chǎocài.now I’m starting to cook the dish.
我先放一点油。wǒ xiān fàng yì diǎn yóu.i’ll add a little oil first.
再加一点盐。zài jiā yì diǎn yán.add a little more salt.
搅拌几分钟。jiǎobàn jǐ fēnzhōng.stir for a few minutes.
盛到盘子里。chéng dào pánzi lǐ.Put into the plate.
我来尝一尝。wǒ lái cháng yi cháng.Let me have a taste
做好了！可以出锅了。zuò hǎo le! kěyǐ chū guō le.Finished! It’s ready to be served.
